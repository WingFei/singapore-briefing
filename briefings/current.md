## ON THE RADAR

### MONETARY POLICY · MAS
### Wednesday’s decision now sits between bank relief and imported inflation

**WATCH · DECISION DUE 14 OCTOBER.** MAS will publish its Monetary Policy Statement and Macroeconomic Review on Wednesday. All 10 economists surveyed by Reuters expect the central bank to increase the rate of appreciation of the S$NEER band, but that remains a forecast. MAS’s July decision left the slope, width and centre unchanged after April’s slight increase in the slope. [MAS release calendar](https://www.mas.gov.sg/monetary-policy/advance-release-calendar) · [Reuters poll · 9 October](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/singapore-poised-tighten-monetary-policy-amid-strong-growth-rising-inflation-2026-10-09/)

The setup is less comfortable than it was last week. Brent settled at US$106.65 on Monday, the US dollar remains firm and haze is still affecting Singapore. Local bank shares rebounded, but the previous week’s S$900 million of net institutional outflows showed how quickly global yields and funding expectations can reset valuations. Monday’s bond market closure leaves Friday’s 5.24% US 10-year yield as the latest completed reference.

**WHY IT MATTERS:** MAS operates through the exchange rate, not a policy interest rate. A steeper appreciation path can restrain imported inflation but may tighten financial conditions for exporters and rate-sensitive assets. Tomorrow’s language on core inflation and growth will matter as much as the mechanical band setting.

**WATCH NEXT:** slope, width and centre of the S$NEER band; MAS’s inflation forecasts; and any change to the assessment of external demand, energy prices and domestic cost pressure.

## TECH & AI

### TECH · AI INFRASTRUCTURE
### Microsoft takes a million-chip route around Nvidia

**MORE THAN 1 MILLION · potential 2027 deployment.** Microsoft launched the Maia 300, the third generation of its custom AI accelerator, and said it could deploy more than one million units next year for internal AI services. This is an announced product and deployment plan, not a completed rollout. [Reuters · 12 October, 9:01 PM SGT](https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-plans-huge-deployment-its-own-ai-chips-with-an-eye-saving-costs-2026-10-12/)

The Maia 300 uses a chiplet design: 2-nanometre TSMC technology handles the central computing functions, while cheaper 3-nanometre components move data around the system. Microsoft said each rack will contain 96 chips and consume about 167 kilowatts, compared with 142 kilowatts for Nvidia’s latest GB300 rack. Microsoft claims roughly 40% more useful computing per megawatt than unnamed leading GPU systems; that efficiency comparison is the company’s claim, not an independent benchmark.

Maia’s predecessor already runs Microsoft’s internal models and OpenAI models served through Azure. The new design aims to reduce dependence on Nvidia, lower cost per unit of useful work and give Microsoft tighter control over the full rack. The company plans multi-gigawatt deployments and expects more than US$175 billion of capital expenditure this calendar year.

**WHY IT MATTERS · Analysis:** custom accelerators change bargaining power across chips, cloud pricing and data-centre design. For Singapore, the relevant constraints are power efficiency, cooling, network capacity and whether regional Azure customers can access Maia-backed services at competitive prices. Watch independent performance tests and the gap between announced capacity and operating deployment.
