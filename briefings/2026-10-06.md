Immediate activity decisions should use one-hour PM2.5, rather than the rolling PSI alone. Schools, employers and cyclists face continuing disruption. **2. Digitalisation supports jobs across sectors.** IMDA’s report, released 5 October and reported by CNA at 3:13 PM, puts the digital economy at **19.3% of GDP**, growing **5.6%** in 2025. Technology employment rose **3.8%**; AI, data and cybersecurity roles grew fastest. The opportunity extends beyond technology companies, strengthening the case for workplace training. [CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/digital-economy-ai-adoption-2025-singapore-imda-6432161)

**3. Workplace safety enforcement intensifies.** MOM announced on 5 October a voluntary safety time-out for construction, transport and storage firms through **18 October**, after seven deaths since September. Enhanced enforcement lasts until **4 November**. First-offence composition fines rise to **S$3,000** and minimum stop-work orders to eight weeks, increasing the operational consequences of safety lapses. The CNA page’s publication time is unavailable; the underlying announcement predates the cutoff. [CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/workplace-deaths-mom-safety-time-out-construction-transport-storage-6432756)

**4. Parliament scrutinises employment and transport.** Today’s questions cover **4,620 Q2 retrenchments**, the North-South Corridor’s two-year delay and property purchases near planned MRT stations. Answers could clarify worker support and construction disruption. The property study is an unreviewed working paper; misconduct has not been established. This is the agenda, not today’s parliamentary outcome. [CNA, listed 5 October; exact publication time unavailable](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/property-purchases-civil-servants-parliament-retrenchments-north-south-corridor-6432476)

**5. Social-service funding expands.** Community Chest reported on 5 October that more than **S$80 million** supported 340 programmes in FY2025, benefiting over 130,000 individuals and families. **S$113 million** in multi-year commitments provides agencies greater planning certainty. These are prior-year outcomes disclosed yesterday, rather than new spending today. [CNA, 7:30 PM](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/community-chest-channels-80-million-sgd-340-social-programmes-6429071)
